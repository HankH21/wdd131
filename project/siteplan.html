Hank's Auto

Used Car Website Planning Document

Site Name

Hank's Auto

Hank's Auto is a website for people who are looking for used cars. I chose this name because "Hank's" gives the website a personal and friendly identity, while "Auto" clearly shows that the website is related to cars.

Site Purpose

Hank's Auto provides information about used cars to help people find a vehicle that fits their needs and budget. Visitors can browse available vehicles, filter cars by different options, and view basic information such as price, year, mileage, and vehicle type.

Scenarios

  • What used cars are available within my budget?
  • Which used cars have low mileage and are suitable for my needs?

Color Schema

Dark Blue (#12304A): Used for the header, main headings, and important text.

Light Gray (#F2F4F5): Used for the main background and content sections.

Orange (#E67E22): Used as an accent color for buttons, links, and other important elements.

Typography

Poppins

Used for the website logo and headings such as h1, h2, and h3.

Open Sans

Used for body text, vehicle information, buttons, and other general content.
